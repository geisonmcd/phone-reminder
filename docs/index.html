Smart Random Reminder

Smart Random Reminder is an Android app that helps you keep short, intentional reminders on your phone without turning them into a complicated task manager. Create reminders in seconds, choose how often each one should appear, pick the days that can receive reminders, and keep the schedule inside the hours that work for you. The app surfaces your reminders as random local notifications during the day, stores them on your device, and offers optional plain-text export/import and Google Drive backup and sync.

Em português: lembretes curtos e intencionais, exibidos como notificações aleatórias ao longo do dia, com seus dados guardados no próprio aparelho.

Get it on Google Play

Features

  • Simple reminder list with fast editing
  • Per-reminder frequency and weekday controls
  • Default notification hours for the whole app
  • Plain-text export and import
  • Optional backup and sync with your own Google Drive
  • No account required, no ads, no behavior analytics
